# Insights and Reporting

This folder contains the main findings, insights, and reporting materials for the Medicare Healthcare Cost and Utilization Analysis project.

## Purpose

The purpose of this section is to translate the analysis results into clear healthcare business insights and recommendations.

## What This Section Will Include

- Preliminary findings
- Cost driver analysis
- Utilization analysis
- Chronic condition insights
- Beneficiary population insights
- Final recommendations
- Executive summary

## Key Questions to Answer

- Which chronic conditions are associated with the highest total healthcare cost?
- Which beneficiary groups have the highest utilization?
- Are costs driven more by claim volume or average claim cost?
- How do inpatient and outpatient costs compare?
- What recommendations can be made based on the results?
